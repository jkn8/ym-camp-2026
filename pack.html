Pack List

Boys hike with a day-pack only. All overnight gear (tent, sleeping bag, food, etc.) goes in the Jeep and trailer to camp. You'll have your full kit at camp Monday afternoon.

Write your name on everything with a Sharpie. Especially water bottles, jackets, headlamps, and anything that can wander off in a shared camp.

Critical Items

  • Day pack for the hike in
  • Sunscreen SPF 50+, 12 oz minimum (sun is intense at altitude)
  • Water bottle or bladder, 2 liters minimum
  • Water filter (Sawyer / Katadyn / similar) — leaders carry backups
  • Hiking shoes — above-ankle, broken in, sturdy tread
  • Personal medications (insulin, EpiPen, inhaler, prescription meds)
  • Mess kit — fork + spoon required, plate / cup optional
  • Small garbage bag for personal trash
  • Headlamp / flashlight with fresh batteries
  • Bug repellant with DEET 30+ (mosquitoes are aggressive)

Clothing

  • 2 lightweight shirts (consider 1 long-sleeve)
  • 2 pairs shorts (consider 1 lightweight pants)
  • 1 mid-weight sweatshirt or fleece (nights are cold)
  • 1 rain / wind jacket
  • 3 pairs socks (wool or synthetic — no cotton)
  • 3 pairs underwear
  • Swimsuit (yes, you'll get in the lake)
  • Hat with brim (for the sun)
  • Beanie (for cold nights)
  • Sunglasses
  • Camp shoes / sandals (Chacos, Crocs, Tevas)

Sleeping

  • Tent — coordinate with your tent-mates ahead of time
  • Sleeping bag rated for 30°F or colder
  • Sleeping pad (foam or inflatable)
  • Pillow (optional — your stuff sack works)

Toiletries

  • Toothbrush + toothpaste
  • Deodorant
  • Hand sanitizer
  • Small pack towel
  • Toilet paper + small trowel (Leave No Trace)
  • Chapstick with SPF
  • Wet wipes (for hygiene — pack out)

Optional but Worth It

  • Fishing pole + tackle (brookies / golden trout in the lake)
  • Utah fishing license (age 12+)
  • Frisbee, football, deck of cards
  • Camera (real one — leave the phone)
  • Notebook + pen (testimony night)
  • Scriptures

Do NOT Bring

Leave these at home:
  • Phones — no signal anyway, and they break and get wet. Leaders have one for emergencies.
  • Hairspray, cologne, scented anything — attracts bugs and bears.
  • Game systems — you're outside.
  • Food in your tent — ever. All food locks in the trailer overnight.

What Leaders Will Provide

  • All breakfast / lunch / dinner meals (4 days)
  • Cooking gear (Blackstone griddles, stoves, pots, utensils)
  • Group first aid kit + tourniquet
  • Water filtration backup
  • Bear spray
  • SPOT GPS for emergencies
  • Paddle boards, kayaks, inflatable boat
  • Archery, BB guns, axes / knives for the activity rotation
  • Telescoping lights for camp at night

Money

Bring $5–10 in cash for the Monday gas-station stop if you want a snack or drink on the way up. After that, no money needed — all food is covered.
