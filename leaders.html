The Operating Model

Each leader is the decision-maker in their area. Route or medical? Ask Zach. Gear, vehicles, or activities at camp? Ask Jared. Food and the Costco run? Ask Andrew. Bishopric / ward coordination and the camp kitchen? Ask Tim. Fast answers, no bottlenecks.

Adult Coverage at All Times

At least two adults are always with each group activity. Boys are never with one adult alone — period. This is the two-deep rule and it's not negotiable.

For parents: if you'd prefer a specific leader to know something about your son (medical, emotional, behavioral), tell that leader directly. We'll respect privacy and act on it. We'd rather know than not.

Bishop & Stake Coordination

The Bishop has been briefed on the trip plan. Stake YM advisor coordination handled by the Bishop Liaison. Any parent who wants to speak with the Bishop directly about the trip should reach out via the standard ward channels.
