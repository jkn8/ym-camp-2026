Ordering

How it works

  1. Leaders finalize the design.
  2. Sizes collected via the RSVP form.
  3. Each YM, each adult leader, and any sibling who wants one gets sized.
  4. Ward covers cost for YM and leaders. Family covers cost for additional siblings / parents.
  5. Orders close June 15 (4 weeks before camp).
  6. Hoodies arrive ~2 weeks later, distributed at the parent meeting before camp.

Sizes

Available: Youth M, L, XL · Adult S, M, L, XL, 2XL.

If you're between sizes, order up — hoodies shrink slightly in the first wash, and these get a lot of years on them.

Hats?
