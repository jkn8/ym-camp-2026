Proposed Menu

A starting point — boys will vet and tweak. Cooked on Blackstone + Camp Chef + backpacking stoves. Calorie-heavy on purpose: you'll be hiking, swimming, surviving overnight, and the altitude burns through food.

Boys: this is a draft. Bring opinions to the next YM meeting. Swap meals you don't love, propose meals you do. The only fixed pieces are the freeze-dried meals on survival night (logistics) and Monday's gas-station-or-eat-at-home breakfast.

Monday — Hike-in Day

July 13
BreakfastAt home before leaving · or buy at the gas station stop ($5–10 cash)
LunchOn the trail. Each boy carries: PB&J or turkey wrap, chips, granola bar, apple, electrolyte drink mix
DinnerWalking Tacos. Browned ground beef, Doritos / Fritos bags, shredded cheese, lettuce, salsa, sour cream. Boys assemble in the chip bag. Hot cocoa after.

Tuesday — Activities + Survival Night

July 14
BreakfastPancake breakfast on the Blackstone. Boys cook. Pancakes, bacon, scrambled eggs, OJ.
LunchBuild-your-own sandwich bar. Turkey, ham, cheese, lettuce, tomato, condiments, chips, fruit, granola bars.
DinnerSteak night. Tri-tip or ribeye on the Blackstone + foil-baked potatoes + corn on the cob + green salad. Banana boats over the fire if allowed.
Survival OvernightFreeze-dried meals. Each group of 2–3 boys gets one Mountain House / Backpacker's Pantry dinner + one breakfast for the morning. Hot chocolate packets + instant coffee for the leaders doing checks.

Wednesday — Deacons Arrive

July 15
BreakfastFrench toast + sausage. Survival groups return to main camp. Refuel.
LunchNacho bar / Walking Tacos round 2. Welcome meal for the deacons. Quick, hot, hands-on. Add a fruit tray.
DinnerSpaghetti night. Spaghetti + meat sauce + garlic bread + Caesar salad. Dutch oven peach cobbler with vanilla ice cream if it survives the cooler.

Thursday — Hike Out

July 16
BreakfastBreakfast burritos. Use up the eggs, sausage, cheese, tortillas. Salsa, hot sauce. Easy cleanup.
Lunch (on the road)Leftover wraps + chips + drinks from the cooler. Eat in the vehicles on the way home.

Snacks (Free-Flow Throughout)

🥨 At camp

  • Granola bars (variety pack)
  • Beef jerky / Slim Jims
  • Trail mix + Goldfish
  • Apples, oranges, bananas
  • Cookies / Oreos / Chips Ahoy
  • S'mores supplies (graham, marshmallow, Hershey's)

🥤 Drinks

  • Water (lots — bring your bottle)
  • Gatorade / Powerade powder
  • Electrolyte tabs (Nuun / LMNT)
  • Hot cocoa packets
  • Instant coffee (for leaders)
  • Capri-Suns or juice boxes

Allergy / Dietary Notes

If your son has a food allergy or dietary restriction (peanut, dairy, gluten, vegetarian, etc.), tell us on the RSVP form. Andrew will adjust the Costco run accordingly. We always have a backup option.

What Leaders Will Be Cooking With

  • Tim's Camp Chef stove (2-burner propane)
  • Jared's Blackstone griddle (large flat-top)
  • Backpacking stoves for survival night
  • Coolers (large, Yeti-class) for cold storage
  • Dutch oven (cobbler, side dishes)
