Shuttle logistics: Jeep capacity is ~4 boys per trip. With 6 boys + leaders you're looking at 2 shuttle runs each direction. Pre-position the Jeep at Hoover before the boys arrive — drop gear at camp first, then drive back up to Hoover. Speaker C also has a Jeep — running two simultaneously cuts trips in half.
Comms at Hoover: no cell signal. Set a hard "we'll be at Hoover by X o'clock" rendezvous time, and a fallback plan if anyone is late or hurt. Two-way radios or SPOT GPS check-ins recommended.
