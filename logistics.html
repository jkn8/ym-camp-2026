Logistics

All the operational details — drop-off, vehicles, food, costs. If you only read one page as a parent, it's Safety. If you read two, this is the second.

Drop-off & Pickup

Monday departure
09:00 at Tim's house. Address shared with families directly
Thursday return
Boys dropped off at their homes as we come back through the valley · approximate window 14:00–17:00 · Final ETA shared by text day-of
What to bring Monday
Filled water bottle, breakfast (or cash for the gas stop), all packed gear, signed permission form if not already returned, any prescription meds in original bottles labeled with name.

Vehicles & Drive Route

Jared's Jeep + open trailer

Drives in via Murdock Basin Road (FR 137) with all the heavy gear — tents, food, paddle boards, Camp Chef, Blackstone, etc. Same Jeep does shuttle duty from Hoover Lake to camp after gear is dropped.

Andrew's Jeep

Second shuttle vehicle. With both Jeeps running in parallel, one shuttle trip moves all the boys from Hoover Lake to camp.

Tim's enclosed trailer (TBD)

Tim may bring an enclosed trailer for food storage — depends on the road condition. If he does, that's the bear-safe food locker. If not, see "Food Storage" below.

Wednesday deacons transport

A separate set of leaders drives the deacons up Wednesday morning via Murdock Basin Road. They do not hike.

The Hike + Shuttle

Monday in:

  1. Boys hike Fehr Lake Trailhead → Hoover Lake (~1.55 mi, ~450 ft descent, real GPX on the Map)
  2. Both Jeeps meet at Hoover Lake — snacks, water refills
  3. Single shuttle trip on Hoover Lake Road to Echo Lake camp (~3.25 mi, ~30 min driving with two Jeeps in parallel)

Thursday out:

  1. Jeeps shuttle the boys back up to Hoover Lake
  2. Boys hike out to Fehr Lake Trailhead (~1.55 mi uphill, ~450 ft gain, light loads only)

Food Plan

Who does what

  • Andrew owns the Costco / Sam's Club run.
  • Tim bought a Camp Chef and runs the camp kitchen.
  • Jared brings the Blackstone, hauls the food in via Jeep + trailer.

Meal coverage

  • Monday breakfast: eat at home or buy at the gas station stop (~10:30)
  • Monday lunch through Thursday breakfast: all provided
  • Survival Tuesday: freeze-dried meals for the overnight

How it's cooked

Blackstone griddle + Camp Chef stove at main camp, plus backpacking stoves for survival night. Boys cook a couple of the breakfasts. Leaders cook bigger dinners. Cleanup is shared.

Proposed menu

See the proposed menu. Boys will vet and tweak it.

Food Storage at Camp

Priority order, depending on what trailer makes it up the road:

  1. Tim's enclosed trailer (preferred) — best bear-safe option.
  2. Far from camp (50+ yards downwind) in sealed containers.
  3. Hung in a tree (bear bag, 12+ ft up, 6+ ft from trunk).

No food in tents, ever. Personal gear stays in tents. Day-pack stays with you on activities.

Drive Time

  • Vineyard → trailhead via UT-150: ~2.5 hours
  • Vineyard → camp via Murdock Basin Rd (Jeep): ~3.5 hours (the road is slow)
  • Murdock Basin Road itself: ~1 hour for 5.25 miles. Rocky 4WD. Don't try it in a car.

Permits & Fees

  • Mirror Lake Highway pass: $6 / 3 days per vehicle (or use an America the Beautiful pass). Buy in Kamas at the ranger station or self-serve along UT-150.
  • Wilderness permit: not required for groups our size.
  • Fishing license: Utah DWR. Required for age 12+.
